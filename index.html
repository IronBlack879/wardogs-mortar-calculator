How to get the coordinates
  1. Open the map and center it on your weapon's position, then on the target.
  2. Right-click and select mark coordinates to open the chat.
  3. Press Ctrl+A to select all, then Ctrl+C to copy.
  4. Paste into the matching field, then repeat for the second point.
  5. Align your sight on the diamond marker and set the RNG to the displayed value.

All calculations run locally on your device. Nothing is transmitted.
